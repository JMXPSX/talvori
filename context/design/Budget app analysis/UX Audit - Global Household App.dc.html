Talvori — how the build holds up, and where the design must move

A code-level review of the Expo / React Native / Supabase codebase (the Talvori design direction), read against the product's own spec pack — plus a verdict on the six concept mockups in UI.png. Companion files: modernized redesign mockups and a summary deck.

Method — static review of 40+ screens/components + spec docs 00–11
Scope — IA · flows · visual system · web/desktop · money & i18n · accessibility
Findings — 32 (5 high · 15 medium · 12 low)
01

Executive summary

The engineering foundation is unusually good for an MVP: one token file reskins the whole app, money lives in minor units, every string is a locale key, navigation adapts by viewport width (not platform), and grocery lists sync in realtime. The bones are right.

The experience layer hasn't caught up. Three patterns hold it back: screens as forms (create/edit forms parked at the foot of list screens instead of dedicated sheets), navigation that hides the product's differentiators (retail prices, coupons and basket compare live three taps deep under "More", while the Budget tab is a three-link stub), and a desktop layout that is mostly a 1,440px-wide phone (only three screens use the bento grid; the rest stretch single columns and full-width forms across the monitor).

Two decisions matter most before beta: give free users a dashboard that informs before it sells (the current first element is a locked paywall hero), and make transactions editable — today the only way to fix a typo is delete and re-enter.

02

What's already strong — keep it

One-file reskin

All color/type/spacing flows from components/theme.ts; every concept in UI.png is reachable by editing tokens, not screens.

Width-based adaptivity

Bottom tabs vs. sidebar decided by viewport (1024px), not OS — the right call for a PWA. RTL flips the sidebar.

Exact money

Minor-unit integers end to end; per-currency formatting; consolidation only through explicit FX snapshots with a visible missing-rates hint.

i18n discipline

en / fil / ar locale files, zero hard-coded strings found in screens, Intl date grouping in the transactions feed.

Realtime where it matters

Grocery lists and items subscribe live — the wife-adds-milk promise from the vision doc actually works.

Guarded destruction

Every delete runs through a confirm sheet; icon buttons carry accessibility labels and hit-slop.

Consistent gating

Premium checks go through one entitlements hook (has()) — the paywall is a policy, not scattered ifs.

Empty/error vocabulary

EmptyState + ErrorNotice components with retry exist — they're just not used everywhere yet (see F19).

05

Findings — home & core flows

F05
HIGH
Free users open to a locked hero. The dashboard's first and largest element for non-premium households is a paywall tile ("Unlock multi-currency… Manage subscription"). The most valuable pixel sells before it informs — a churn pattern, especially at first run.Evidence: app/(tabs)/index.tsx — hero branches on has('multi_currency_dashboard').Fix: free hero shows real balances grouped per currency; only the consolidated single number is premium, upsold as one quiet row beneath. See mockup 1a.
F06
MED
Quick actions mix jobs with admin; recent activity is missing. "Manage FX rates" sits beside Add expense in the top-5 grid, while recent transactions — already fetched for the donut — never render on Home. Every UI.png concept and most competitors lead with recent activity.Evidence: app/(tabs)/index.tsx actions[] and unused txns beyond breakdown.Fix: 4 actions (Income · Expense · Transfer · Compare basket); FX rates moves next to the consolidated total; add a Recent card linking to the feed.
F07
LOW
Donut legend truncates at six categories with no path to the rest.Fix: "+N more" row → category report.
F08
HIGH
Transactions cannot be edited. There is no edit route, no row tap target — fixing an amount or category means delete + re-enter, losing the original timestamp. For a money app this is the single biggest trust gap.Evidence: app/(tabs)/transactions.tsx — rows render a trash Pressable only; finance/entry.tsx creates only.Fix: row tap → detail/edit sheet (reuse entry form with initial values); delete moves inside it.
F09
MED
A trash can on every row. The confirm sheet saves you, but permanent destructive affordances on each transaction and budget row add noise and invite slips — and they're the row's only action.Fix: move delete behind the row's edit sheet (mobile: also swipe-to-delete; web: hover-revealed overflow menu).
F10
MED
No retrieval tools on the feed. No search, no category/account/type filters, no date range, no month in/out summary. Day grouping alone stops scaling after a few weeks of household data.Fix: month header with totals + filter chips; search on web. See mockup 1c.
F11
MED
The grocery list isn't shaped for the store aisle. Adding an item means scrolling past every existing item to a form at the bottom; checkout ("Complete list") is another inline form below that; "Delete list" — the most dangerous action — is the last, most reachable button. This screen is used one-handed, pushing a cart.Evidence: app/grocery/[id].tsx — 400 lines, single ScrollView.Fix: pinned quick-add bar at top, sticky totals + "Finish shopping" CTA, checkout as a sheet, delete into an overflow menu. See mockup 1d.
F12
MED
Marking an item purchased is a wide button plus an always-visible price field. The universal pattern is a checkbox/tick circle; the actual price should be an optional prompt on tick (the field already defaults to estimate).Fix: leading tick target ≥44px, price captured in the tick confirmation toast/sheet.
F13
MED
Create-forms live at the foot of list screens. Household, retailer, and budget-allocation creation render inline under their lists — you scroll past everything to add, and on desktop these forms stretch to full column width.Evidence: household/index.tsx, retail/index.tsx, finance/budgets.tsx.Fix: "+" in the header opening a modal/sheet (pattern already exists: grocery/new, budget-new).
F14
MED
Income/expense entry has no date field — everything posts "now". Households batch-enter receipts at day's end and backfill; that's impossible today.Evidence: finance/entry.tsx — no occurred_at input.Fix: date row defaulting to today (Today / Yesterday / picker).
F15
LOW
Chip clouds won't scale. Accounts and categories render as wrap-around chip walls in entry, checkout, and allocation forms — fine at 5, unusable at 15+ categories.Fix: keep chips for ≤6; otherwise a select row opening a searchable sheet.
06

Findings — visual system

F16
MED
Two card systems. A ui/Card component exists, yet ≥5 screens hand-roll their own styles.card with copy-pasted padding/shadow/radius (grocery, retail, household, subscription, compare). They already drift.Fix: delete local card styles; Card takes a style override where needed.
F17
MED
Danger has no variant. "Delete list" paints a primary Button red via style override; elsewhere destructive actions are bare red text links. Three different vocabularies for the same meaning.Fix: Button variant="danger" (+ quiet text-danger variant); reserve filled red for confirm sheets.
F18
LOW
Three page scaffolds coexist — Screen, BentoPage, and hand-rolled SafeAreaView+ScrollView (the same max-width comment is pasted 8×). Titles sometimes live in the screen, sometimes in the stack header.Fix: one Page scaffold with a title/actions slot; BentoPage becomes a layout child.
F19
LOW
Empty states split personalities. Home and Transactions use the proper EmptyState (icon + message + CTA); grocery, household, and retail show a bare muted sentence.Fix: EmptyState everywhere — first-run moments are onboarding, not dead ends.
F20
LOW
A gap in the type ramp. List-row titles borrow the 24px "heading" (grocery lists, retailers, compare columns) because nothing sits between body-16 and heading-24 — rows shout.Fix: add a 18–19px "subheading" token; headings stay for page/tile titles.
07

Findings — web & large screen

F21
HIGH
Most of the app is a 1,440px-wide phone on desktop. Only Home, Transactions and Budgets compose BentoRows. Grocery, retail, household, subscription, entry, compare and every auth screen stretch a single column — with form fields and buttons spanning ~1,360px. The PWA is the stated Windows/macOS strategy, so this is the product on desktop.Evidence: CONTENT_MAX_WIDTH = 1440 used as a raw cap in 8 screens; Bento.tsx used in 3.Fix: forms cap at ~480px; hub screens get 2–3 column bento; reading columns cap at ~720px. See mockup 1e.
F22
MED
No hover, no visible keyboard focus. Pressables style only the pressed state; on web there is no cursor feedback, no hover tint, and no :focus-visible ring — keyboard users navigate blind.Fix: hover + focus ring in the shared primitives (Button, ListRow, Chip, rows) once — RN-web supports hovered/focused state props.
F23
MED
No persistent top bar on wide layouts. Titles, the add button, and household context scroll away with content; stack screens (finance/*, grocery/*) fall back to a different native-header chrome than tab screens — two headers vocabularies on one site.Fix: wide layout = sidebar + fixed top bar (title · household switcher · global "+").
F24
LOW
Data viz stays phone-sized on monitors. The donut is fixed at 140px; desktop bento cells could carry a 240px chart with full legend, trends, and per-member splits.Fix: size charts from container width; desktop earns the Insights ideas from UI.png option 6.
08

Findings — money & internationalization

F25
MED
Reporting currency is a free-typed 3-letter field. Creating a household asks the user to type "PHP" from memory (device default helps). A typo mints a broken household; the field gates every future number.Evidence: household/index.tsx TextField autoCapitalize="characters".Fix: searchable currency picker with symbol + name, device-locale default preselected.
F26
MED
Colliding currency symbols go undisambiguated. The feed prints each amount in its own currency — correct — but USD, AUD, SGD, NZD (four launch markets) all render "$". A mixed feed becomes guesswork.Fix: when a household holds >1 currency, format with code (US$ / A$ / S$ or "1,234.00 SGD").
F27
MED
Raw ISO dates + a hard-coded LTR arrow. Budget cards print "2026-01-01 → 2026-01-31" — developer formatting, and the "→" doesn't flip for Arabic RTL (spec names real RTL readiness a requirement).Evidence: finance/budgets.tsx period line.Fix: Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatRange with the app locale.
F28
LOW
FX freshness is invisible on the number it affects. The consolidated hero shows a total with no "as of" for the rates behind it — the spec's own principle is "price freshness must be visible". The missing-rates hint is good; staleness needs the same honesty.Fix: "≈ ₱182,400 · rates as of Aug 18" caption linking to /finance/rates.
F29
LOW
Decimal-comma input likely fails. Amount fields parse with Number()-style conversion; a user whose keyboard emits "1.234,56" or "12,50" gets a validation error with no guidance. Verify against lib/money.ts and add localized parsing (or a currency-aware amount input).
09

Findings — accessibility

F30
MED
Selection chips have no semantics. Account/category chips are bare Pressables — no role, no selected state — so screen readers announce plain text; selection is communicated by fill color alone.Evidence: entry.tsx / grocery/[id].tsx inline chips (the shared Chip component isn't used there either — see F16's pattern).Fix: accessibilityRole="radio"/"checkbox" + state on one shared Chip; add a non-color cue (check glyph).
F31
MED
Charts carry no non-visual summary. The donut is decorative SVG; the legend helps but isn't announced as a unit ("Groceries 24%, ₱4,320 of ₱18,000 total").Fix: accessibilityLabel on the chart summarizing top slices + total.
F32
LOW
Primary money data at 12px. Legend amounts, budget spent/limit lines and grocery prices use the caption token; the trash target (18px icon + slop) lands ~42px, just under the 44px floor.Fix: money never below 13–14px with tabular numerals; lift icon targets to 44px.
10

The six concept options (UI.png)

Shown in original color — the palettes are the evidence.

Two verdicts apply to all six before judging any one: every concept is phone-only — none answers the desktop/PWA question that the platform strategy makes primary — and every concept leads with "spent this month vs. budget", which is emotionally more useful than the shipped app's total-balance hero. That heroes swap is worth stealing regardless of theme.

1 · Modern clean
Closest to what's built. Safest evolution of the Talvori direction; its segmented Overview/Budget/Transactions header duplicates the tab bar — drop that.
2 · Dark mode
A theme, not a design. The token file makes it cheap later; doubling contrast QA now isn't MVP work. Ship as a setting post-beta.
3 · Pastel + mascot
Undermines trust for a money product handling remittances; pink-on-pink text will fail contrast at these sizes. Keep only the idea of encouragement — as microcopy, not a mascot.
4 · Card / Material
Reads as 2019 Material with a teal header wave; the 2×2 action grid pushes content below the fold. Conflicts with the established identity — pass.
5 · Minimal / Focus
Its restraint and "days left" stat are worth keeping; its menu-list body duplicates navigation (same trap as the current Budget tab).
6 · Insights
Best ideas to steal. Trend vs. last month, avg/day, transaction count — this is the premium analytics screen (and the desktop bento's best tenant). Not a base theme.

Recommendation: stay on the Talvori identity (option 1's territory), adopt the spent-vs-budget hero and option 6's insight stats, and spend the saved effort on the desktop layout none of the six addresses.
