Global-first promise, six leaks

Currency typed by hand — "PHP" in a free-text field gates every number. → searchable picker.
"$" means four launch markets — USD/AUD/SGD/NZD collide in mixed feeds. → show codes when >1 currency.
"2026-01-01 → 2026-01-31" — raw ISO + an arrow that won't flip in Arabic. → Intl.formatRange.
Decimal commas rejected — "12,50" fails numeric parsing with no guidance. → localized amount input.
Chips without semantics — selection is color-only, unannounced. → role + state + check glyph on one shared Chip.
Money at 12px — legend and budget amounts use the caption token. → 14px floor, tabular numerals.

The six concepts: keep the identity, steal two ideas

1 · Modern clean — closest to built
Safest evolution; drop its duplicate header tabs.
2 · Dark mode
A theme, not a design — cheap later via tokens. Post-beta setting.
3 · Pastel + mascot
Undermines trust for remittance money; contrast fails. Keep only encouraging microcopy.
4 · Card / Material
2019 Material wave header; fights the Talvori identity. Pass.
5 · Minimal / focus
Keep the restraint + "days left" stat; its menu body repeats navigation.
6 · Insights — steal the stats
Trend, avg/day, count → the premium analytics screen and desktop tiles.
All six are phone-only and all six lead with spent-vs-budget — adopt that hero; spend the saved effort on desktop, which none of them answers.
